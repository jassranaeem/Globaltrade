Questions

        
Importing a car from Japan, answered

      

      

        
How do I import a car from Japan with Global Trade?
Send us the make, model, year and budget. We search Japanese auctions, send you the translated auction sheet and photos, bid only with your approval, then handle export documents, shipping and delivery to your port.

        
What is a Japanese auction sheet grade?
Every auction car is inspected and given an overall grade (S, 6, 5, 4.5, 4, 3.5, 3, R/RA) and an interior grade (A to E). Grade 4.5 or higher with interior A or B is excellent condition.

        
Which countries do you ship cars to?
Pakistan, UAE, UK, Kenya, Tanzania, Sri Lanka, New Zealand, Ireland and more. We check your country's age, engine and steering rules before we bid.

        
How long does it take to get my car?
Finding and buying usually takes 1 to 3 weeks depending on the model. Shipping time depends on your port and the vessel schedule, and we share tracking once the car is loaded.
