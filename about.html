About Global Trade

Your buying team inside Japan

株式会社 Global Trade is a trading company based in Ibaraki Prefecture, Japan. We help people around the world buy the Japanese car they want, with the same information a local buyer would have.

Our story

Why we started Global Trade

Buying a car from another country is a big decision, and most buyers only see a few photos and a price. We started Global Trade to change that. We work from Japan, close to the auction halls of the Kanto region, and we act for the buyer, not the seller.

That means we look for the car you asked for, not the car we want to sell. We show you the auction sheet and explain what it means. And we only bid when you say yes.

What we do

We source used cars from Japanese auctions and dealers, check their inspection records, buy them on the customer's behalf, prepare the export documents and ship them to ports in Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Europe and Oceania.

Who we work with

Private buyers importing a car for themselves, and small dealers who want a reliable buying partner in Japan.

How we work

Four promises we keep on every car

Transparency

You see what we see

Every option comes with the original auction sheet, its translation and clear photos. If a sheet shows a problem, we tell you before you ask.

Your approval

No bid without your yes

You choose the car and the maximum price. We never go above your limit, and we never buy a car you did not approve.

Clear costs

A price you can check

We break the price into auction price, fees, transport, documents and freight, so you can compare it with any local offer.

Right car, right rules

Checked for your country

Before bidding, we confirm your country's age, engine and steering rules so the car can be cleared and registered.

Based in Japan

Why it matters that we are in Japan

Being on the ground means we can attend auctions, check cars in person when needed, deal with Japanese paperwork directly and move cars to the right port quickly.

It also means you talk to one team for the whole process, in English, on WhatsApp or email.
